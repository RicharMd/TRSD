You are an expert in Large Language Model (LLM) alignment, prompt engineering, and knowledge distillation.

### Context & Goal
We are implementing a Self-Distillation framework.
- The Student model only receives the raw question: `$orig_content`.
- The Teacher model receives a prompt template that contains both the raw question (`$orig_content`) and a high-quality reference response (`$output_text`) to guide its output distribution.

We want to optimize the "wrapper" text around these two variables to find the optimal prompt that minimizes the KL-divergence between the Teacher and a baseline target.

### Your Task
Generate 8 distinct, highly professional English prompt templates for the Teacher model.

### Critical Constraint
Every template MUST preserve the exact placeholders: `$orig_content` and `$output_text`. Do not modify them.

### Design Variations to Explore
Please maximize the diversity of the templates by varying these two dimensions:

1. **Layout & Positioning:** Freely experiment with the placement of the placeholders.
   - E.g., placing `$orig_content` at the beginning, in the middle, or at the very end.
   - E.g., placing `$output_text` as a prefix context or as a concluding example.
2. **Pedagogical Persona:**
   - **Analytical:** Force the teacher to dissect the underlying logic of the reference.
   - **Strict Adherence:** Emphasize rigid compliance with the format and style of the reference.
   - **Encouraging/Soft:** Use a relaxed, advisory tone to reduce the rigidity of the output distribution.

### Expected Output Format
Please output ONLY pure text. Separate each of the 8 variants clearly using the delimiter `=== TEMPLATE X ===`, and provide a 1-sentence description of the strategy used before the actual template text.

Example format:

=== TEMPLATE 1 ===
Strategy: Analytical persona with the question placed at the very end.

[Your raw text containing $orig_content and $output_text here]

=== TEMPLATE 2 ===
Strategy: ...
